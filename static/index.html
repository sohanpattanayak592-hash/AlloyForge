Metallurgical & Shop-Floor Notes

The linear programming charge model calculates the minimum-cost ferro-alloy addition recipe satisfying all ASTM/EN element tolerances. Recovery rates account for typical induction furnace burn-off (98% Ni, 96% Mo, 90% Cr, 85% Mn, 82% Si). If carbon or silicon in scrap exceeds the grade maximum, decarburization via oxygen lancing or low-carbon steel dilution is recommended before tapping.
